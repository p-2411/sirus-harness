## Reddit — revised body

Your AI subscriptions, together in one terminal.

I'm building Sirus for people who already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or multiple accounts and want to use that access in one place. Connect your existing subscriptions, including more than one account from the same provider. Add API keys too if that's part of your setup.

From there, you can switch models in a session, give different participants their own models, build agent teams across providers, and carry project memory into later sessions. Having one model implement and another review is one example of what that shared workspace enables.

A first session I'd suggest:
1. Install Sirus and open a project you know.
2. Use /login for each subscription account you want to connect, then /model to choose your starting model.
3. Bring one real task. Switch models or add another participant when useful, with the earlier conversation available.

It's open source under MIT, with no separate Sirus account. Your provider subscriptions remain separate: their charges and usage limits still apply. Sessions and memories are stored locally; model requests go to the provider you select.

Demo examples and install steps: https://trysirus.com/?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=first10&utm_content=sideproject

I'm looking for the first people to try their existing setup in Sirus. Which subscriptions or accounts are you juggling, and what would you most want to keep together?
