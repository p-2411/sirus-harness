# PeerPush listing review

Checked 7 September 2026 using public official pages. No sign-in, account creation, submission, or external message performed by this reviewer.

## Recommendation

Submit Sirus through the free publishing queue after the browser agent checks for an existing listing and verifies the current form. This is a suitable directory for a terminal coding tool and has specific matching categories. Budget remains $0; do not select instant publishing, promotion, or the Outrank board.

## Submission route and cost

- [Pricing](https://peerpush.com/pricing) explicitly offers **Free, $0**, with a Submit for Free link and a queue behind paid users.
- That link leads to [Submit](https://peerpush.com/submit), which publicly redirects to [login with return to submit](https://peerpush.com/auth/login?redirectTo=/submit).
- Paid choices currently shown are $39 instant launch, $89 seven-day promotion, and $229 thirty-day promotion. The Outrank board starts at $5. None is required by the public free offer.
- [Homepage FAQ](https://peerpush.com/) says submissions can be filled manually or automatically from a URL; free submissions enter a publishing queue. It lists descriptions, use cases, audiences, pricing, platforms, alternatives and screenshots as listing content. Prefer manually checked copy, since auto-fill may exaggerate or misclassify Sirus.

## Account and listing rules

[Terms](https://peerpush.com/terms) specify a valid email and either email magic-link authentication or Google OAuth. They require accurate product information, authority to submit, a functional product accessible from its website, and original/licensed screenshots and logos. Submitted content remains owned by its author, with permission for PeerPush to display/promote it; published content may survive account deletion. Automated click/vote/boost manipulation and false information are prohibited. Sirus does not fall into the listed prohibited product categories. This review establishes no entitlement to make claims about provider endorsement, quota pooling, measured customer results, or reach.

## Backlink

[Platform Rules](https://peerpush.com/rules) describe a website link back to the PeerPush listing as a verification signal with a small ranking benefit. The public pricing, rules and terms do not make a reciprocal link a prerequisite for the free listing. The final form should still be checked. Do not add a backlink to the Sirus site merely to improve a directory score.

## Duplicate and reach checks

- Public domain searches for Sirus, trysirus, and trysirus.com found no result. The inspected homepage/developer-tools content did not expose Sirus either. This is not an exhaustive duplicate check; use site search before creating the listing.
- The homepage displays its own AI-read and visitor counts. Those are PeerPush marketing claims, not verified Sirus referral traffic, unique interested people, recommendations, installs, or activations. No reach projection is used in this recommendation.
- After submission, record the exact text and distinguish queued/pending from published. A listing does not establish a new user.
