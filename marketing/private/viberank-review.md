# Viberank listing review

Checked 7 September 2026. Public web research only; no account created and no submission made by this reviewer.

## Recommendation

Proceed with the normal free submission route if the live form confirms it. Sirus fits the directory's Developer Tools category, with AI as the secondary category if multiple selections are offered. Use the prepared subscription-first copy in `marketing/directory-listing-pack.md`.

## Verified findings

- [Homepage](https://viberank.dev/) and [Developer Tools category](https://viberank.dev/?category=Developer+Tools) show software tools, including AI and developer products. The homepage has a Search app control.
- [About, usage guidelines and privacy](https://viberank.dev/info) describe community app submissions and weekly/monthly/all-time ranking. Submissions must be accurate, original or licensed, respect third-party rights, and accurately represent the app. Users retain intellectual property and grant a non-exclusive license for display and promotion on the platform.
- The same privacy page says it collects account email/name/profile picture, interaction data and technical information, and uses third-party authentication. Exact sign-in providers and required form fields were not exposed in the public web rendering.
- [Rewards](https://viberank.dev/rewards) says submissions receive three vibes and are reviewed for meaningful value. It also shows a Boost Your Sales control; its price and conditions were not visible. Avoid sponsorship/boost purchase under the campaign's $0 budget.
- [Contact](https://viberank.dev/contact) explicitly welcomes app-submission questions.
- No mandatory reciprocal backlink or fee appeared in these official public pages. This is not confirmation of the live form's terms; the form must still be inspected before submission.
- No Sirus or trysirus match appeared in reviewed homepage/category content or indexed domain search. The directory has pagination, so this does not conclusively rule out an existing listing. Use its Search app control for both terms before creating one.

## Items for the submitting agent to verify in the browser
